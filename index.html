Corporate Profile

Engineering Excellence & Compliance

Delivering certified reliability for wind energy asset owners, IPPs, and turbine operators.

Thendral Wind Tech LLP is a specialized mechanical engineering and maintenance organization headquartered in Tamil Nadu, India. Operating under an accredited Integrated Management System (IMS), we deliver end-to-end diagnostics, up-tower repairs, workshop overhauls, and certified manpower deployment for utility-scale wind turbine gearboxes.

Our engineering framework combines high-precision endoscopic inspection, spectral vibration analysis, and certified quality procedures to eliminate catastrophic mechanical failures and maximize annual energy production (AEP).

Technical Solutions

Comprehensive Gearbox Engineering Services

Certified field diagnostics, mechanical overhaul, and maintenance services across all turbine makes.

Gearbox Endoscopy & Health Inspection

Comprehensive internal inspection using high-resolution video scopes covering sun gears, planetary stages, intermediate gears, and high-speed bearings.

Up-Tower & Workshop Overhauls

In-situ up-tower repairs and full workshop gearbox refurbishment restoring factory tolerances, preload settings, and gear mesh alignment.

Vibration Analysis & Condition Monitoring

Multi-channel spectral vibration diagnostics, structural resonance evaluation, and dynamic balancing to detect early bearing fatigue and tooth micro-pitting.

Lubrication Diagnostics & Offline Filtration

ISO particle count verification, viscosity profiling, wear debris classification, and high-efficiency offline oil flushing.

Precision Bearing Replacement

Controlled induction heating and hydraulic mounting of high-speed and intermediate shaft bearings according to strict manufacturer specifications.

Certified Technical Manpower Support

Deployment of qualified wind turbine service technicians, mechanical engineers, and riggers for planned overhauls and breakdown missions.
